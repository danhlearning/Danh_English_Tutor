# Animals pronunciation audio

50 fixed English (US) word clips for `grade/lop2/animals.html`.

- Synthesized with Piper `en_US-ljspeech-medium` (US female voice, trained on the public-domain LJ Speech dataset). Model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium/MODEL_CARD
- Encoded as mono MP3, 22,050 Hz, 64 kbit/s. Only finished clips are stored here; the 61 MB voice model is not part of the website.
- The IPA labels are American English. Representative words were cross-checked against Cambridge Dictionary US pronunciations, including fox, goat, giraffe, turtle, rhinoceros, hippopotamus, and kangaroo.
- `hippopotamus` and `kangaroo` were regenerated with explicit phonemes to place stress on the appropriate syllable.
- Automated checks cover file existence, MP3 signatures, and selected IPA labels. Human listening review remains necessary before treating every clip as approved teaching audio.
